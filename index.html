About

I'm Mitch Bentz, and I create under the name WubbzP (pronounced Wubbs-P, /ˈwʌbz.piː/ ). I've worked with vocal synthesis software since 2017 and started releasing covers publicly as HuxP in 2023, then rebranded to WubbzP in September 2026.

My music work focuses on VOCALOID and UTAU, especially projects featuring Shiki Taigen.

Outside of music, I maintain archives, build fan and personal websites, and do visual/site design work.

iWubbzy

iWubbzy is an unofficial, non-commercial fan resource for Wow! Wow! Wubbzy!.

I also help run a shared Discord community for updates, events, and discussion centred around the site and my music.

iWubbzy Central A shared Discord server for iWubbzy and WubbzP.
