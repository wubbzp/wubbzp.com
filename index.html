WubbzP

WubbzP
NameMitch Bentz
Also known asHuxP
OccupationsArchiver, Musician, Web Designer
Active years2017 - present
GenresVOCALOID, UTAU

Mitch Bentz, better known online as WubbzP (pronounced Wubbs-P, /ˈwʌbz.piː/ ), is an archiver, visual designer, and creator of vocal synthesis covers. He has worked with vocal synthesis software since 2017. Initially releasing covers publicly under the alias HuxP in 2023, he officially rebranded to WubbzP in September 2026.

This site serves as a centralized hub to share new music releases, ongoing web projects, and the communities he helps run.

About

WubbzP's music work primarily focuses on VOCALOID and UTAU software, specifically notable projects featuring the vocal synth Shiki Taigen. Outside of music production, Bentz maintains digital archives, builds personal and fan websites, and conducts general visual and site design work.

iWubbzy

iWubbzy is an unofficial, non-commercial fan resource dedicated to preserving and celebrating the animated television series Wow! Wow! Wubbzy!.

A shared Discord community is also maintained for updates, events, and discussion centered around the iWubbzy site and WubbzP's music releases.

iWubbzy Central A shared Discord server for iWubbzy and WubbzP.
