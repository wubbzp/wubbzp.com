Controls

  • Draw: click and drag on the canvas, or use a finger or pen.
  • Colours: the round colour button, or A.
  • Tools: brush, eraser, fill and stamps, or 1–4. Click the brush or eraser again to pick a size.
  • Size: ↑ / ↓ (the + and − on the D-pad). Stamps get bigger and smaller too.
  • Undo / Redo: ← / →, or Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y. Five steps, like the original.
  • Pick up a colour: right-click the canvas, or hold Alt and click.
  • Back / Exit: B or Esc. Mute: M.
  • Save keeps up to three pictures in this browser. SD Card downloads a PNG, and loading from it opens a picture from your computer.

A fan recreation of the Art Studio from SpongeBob SquigglePants (THQ / WayForward, 2011) for the uDraw GameTablet, using the game's own art and music. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Nickelodeon, THQ or WayForward.
