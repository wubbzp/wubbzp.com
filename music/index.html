Discography

WubbzP Music is WubbzP's own label. She has made music with vocal synths since 2017, starting with the VOCALOID4 engine, and now works mainly with VOCALOID and UTAU in J-pop, pop and children's music.

Releases from before September 2026 came out under her former name, HuxP.

Voices you'll hear

  • Shiki Taigen
  • Hatsune Miku

About "remastered" editions: they aren't just remasters. They're re-recordings of the covers, usually with different voicebanks from the originals.

Vocarips

  • Album
  • 1st album
  • 13 September 2023

WubbzP's first album. It's usually considered a compilation album of LockedStep works.

Its covers were re-released in 2025 on Vocarips + Vocadiva, and the Deluxe edition's 2 exclusive tracks got their own "remastered" single.

Editions

  • Vocarips 13 September 2023 8 tracks

    The original release.

  • Vocarips (Deluxe) 14 September 2023 10 tracks

    The deluxe edition, released the day after the original.

  • Vocarips (Deluxe) - Bonus Tracks (Remastered) 4 August 2025 2 tracks Single

    A single with "remastered" versions of the 2 tracks that were exclusive to the Deluxe edition.

Vocarips + Vocadiva

  • Compilation
  • 2nd cover compilation
  • 23 April 2025

A 2025 re-release of Vocarips and Vocadiva. It includes the "remastered" (re-recorded) covers and a couple of new covers.

  • Both Vocarips and Vocadiva included a cover of "FREELY TOMORROW", so a bonus track takes the place of the original "remastered" (re-recorded) cover of it.
  • While it's mainly a compilation of "remastered" (re-recorded) covers, tracks 4-8 weren't remastered and use the same audio as their original releases.

Editions

  • Vocarips + Vocadiva 23 April 2025 18 tracks

    The original release.
