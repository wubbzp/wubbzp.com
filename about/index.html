About Me

Wow, wow! Here's everything you need to know about WubbzP!

Hi, I'm WubbzP!

Mitch "Michelle" Bentz, better known online as WubbzP (pronounced Wubbs-P, /ˈwʌbz.piː/ ), is an archiver, visual designer, and creator of vocal synthesis covers.

She began working with the VOCALOID4 engine in 2017. Her music work primarily focuses on VOCALOID and UTAU software, specifically notable projects featuring the vocal synth Shiki Taigen alongside Hatsune Miku. Working mainly in J-pop, pop, and children's music, she releases her music through her own label, WubbzP Music.

She started releasing covers publicly under the name HuxP in 2023, then rebranded to WubbzP in September 2026.

Her discography includes the album Miku + Michelle and its deluxe edition, alongside popular covers such as "Sadistic.Music∞Factory". While known for her extensive cover work, she has stated that her future goals include producing her own original songs.

Outside of music production, Bentz maintains digital archives, builds personal and fan websites, and conducts general visual and site design work.
